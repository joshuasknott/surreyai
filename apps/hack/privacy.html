The interest list

Your email.
A clear purpose.

We use your email to contact you about Surrey AI Hack 2027.

What is saved

The signup form records your email address, the signup time and your agreement to receive event updates. This information is stored privately using Vercel. An automatic signup notification, including your email address, is sent through Resend to ussu.aianddatascience@surrey.ac.uk so the society can manage the interest list. It is not published on this website.

What joining means

You are registering an interest in event announcements. Joining the list does not reserve a place or confirm a ticket. Date, venue, cost and team arrangements will be announced separately.

Questions or removal requests

Contact Surrey AI Society at ussu.aianddatascience@surrey.ac.uk if you have a question about your details or would like your address removed from the list.
