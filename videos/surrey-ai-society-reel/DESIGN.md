# Design System

## Overview

Surrey Artificial Intelligence Society uses a bright technical campus identity: warm off-white surfaces, oversized near-black typography, teal-blue accents, and generous whitespace. The homepage pairs a friendly futuristic robot-head hero with practical project proof, especially dark photographic cards for Racing Simulator and Agentic Builder. Layouts are clear and student-facing, with early join/project actions, split content blocks, and soft bordered cards. The video should preserve this accessible technical feel while increasing motion density for social pacing.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#F7F8F3` - warm off-white homepage background.
- **Clean Surface**: `#FCFCFB` - alternate section surface.
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` - nav, rail, and lightweight card surfaces.
- **Primary Content**: `#172329` - near-black headings and key labels.
- **Body Content**: `#5E6C70` - muted explanatory copy.
- **AI Teal**: `#3F6D80` - primary CTA, labels, links, and accent rules.
- **AI Teal Dark**: `#3B6C80` - active button and stronger teal details.
- **Quiet Border**: `#DCE4E0` - section lines, card edges, and UI dividers.
- **Secondary Border**: `#BFCBC8` - outlined buttons and card borders.
- **Project Dark**: `#0B0F19` - dark overlay language from project cards.
- **Success Signal**: `#10B981` - small live/agent status accent only.

## Typography

- **Primary Font**: Inter. Used across display, headings, body, nav, buttons, and cards.
- **Display Weight**: 850, large stacked titles. Homepage hero uses 71.2px on desktop; video can scale to 92-130px for portrait.
- **Section Heading**: Inter 850, 48px on site; video uses 64-86px depending on beat density.
- **Body**: Inter 400, muted `#5E6C70`, generous line height. Keep video body text at 30px or larger.
- **Labels**: Inter 850, teal `#3F6D80`, compact uppercase or numbered markers.
- **Code / Agent UI**: Fira Code 400/600 for terminal, prompt, and build-step readouts.

## Elevation

Depth is soft and functional. The site uses thin `#DCE4E0` borders, pill buttons, low teal-tinted shadows, and dark image overlays instead of heavy drop shadows. The robot hero sits on a pale grid/backplate, while project cards use cinematic image darkness to make white copy readable.

## Components

- **Robot-Head Hero**: large friendly AI head on a pale technical grid, paired with stacked display typography and early CTAs.
- **Pill CTA Pair**: filled teal primary button with soft teal shadow, outlined secondary pill with quiet border.
- **Quick-Link Rail**: three white bordered cards for Events, Learning, and Projects, used as early navigation proof.
- **Learning Toolkit Split**: study-desk cutout with floating card plus numbered practical-skills list.
- **Project Image Cards**: large rounded dark photographic cards with white overlay text for Racing Simulator and Agentic Builder.
- **Events Programme Block**: plain split section with format chips: Workshops, Hackathons, Project nights, Talks, Socials.
- **Join Strip**: large off-white CTA section with tiny logo mark and a single teal membership action.
- **Agentic Workspace UI**: dark app panel, Fira Code console lines, build-step nodes, and small status controls.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use the robot head as the first unmistakable visual signal.
- Keep join, projects, and events visible early in the story.
- Use `#F7F8F3` and `#FCFCFB` as the main canvas with teal system accents.
- Make project imagery feel cinematic with dark overlays, zooms, and data-line motion.
- Use Fira Code sparingly for agent/build/coding moments.
- Keep the society beginner-friendly by showing workshops, examples, and student project outcomes.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the whole video into a generic neon-on-black AI reel.
- Do not hide the CTA behind a long abstract intro.
- Do not invent event dates, sponsors, awards, statistics, or claims not present on the site.
- Do not use deployment preview URLs or external site captures.
- Do not make text-only beats; every beat needs a site asset or a concrete UI/project visual.
- Do not overuse purple/blue gradients; the brand accent is teal on a warm pale campus canvas.
