What we offer

Surrey AI Society is designed for experienced coders, curious beginners, and students from any discipline who want to understand how artificial intelligence is changing the world.

01

Learn practical skills

Workshops and tutorials help members build confidence with Python, machine learning, data analysis, generative AI, and model evaluation.

02

Build with others

Hackathons, build nights, and project sessions turn ideas into small prototypes, portfolio pieces, and team experiments.

03

Understand the impact

Industry awareness, ethics, careers, accessibility, bias, and social impact stay part of the conversation from the start.
