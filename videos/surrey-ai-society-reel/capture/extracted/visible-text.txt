[h2] What we offer
[p] Surrey AI Society is designed for experienced coders, curious beginners, and students from any discipline who want to understand how artificial intelligence is changing the world.
[strong] Test AI for yourself.
[span] Sessions turn models, datasets, tools, and judgement into shared material members can inspect and improve.
[h3] Learn practical skills
[p] Workshops and tutorials help members build confidence with Python, machine learning, data analysis, generative AI, and model evaluation.
[h3] Build with others
[p] Hackathons, build nights, and project sessions turn ideas into small prototypes, portfolio pieces, and team experiments.
[h3] Understand the impact
[p] Industry awareness, ethics, careers, accessibility, bias, and social impact stay part of the conversation from the start.
